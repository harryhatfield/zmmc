A holistic view on the future of sustainable farming.

What's Inside

Five short sections, one clear argument.

The starting point

Farming was once the cornerstone of every village, town and city: short supply chains, local processing, diverse farms and money that stayed in the community. How that changed, and what was lost.

The framework

Holistic management treats land, livestock, people and money as one whole system. Why that way of thinking points to mixed farming, where each enterprise feeds the next.

The problem

Small abattoirs have been closing across the UK. When local slaughter disappears, only scale survives, and the farm loses its route to its own community.

Our position

Mixed farming needs to return, at small scale, in thousands of places. Why decentralisation is food security, and why poultry is where we have started.

The vision

From poultry to the whole farm, sold locally to the public. The paper sets out proposals for discussion, built on a standard in proportion to the size of the farm, and what it will take from farmers, regulators and the public.

In Short

Farming was the cornerstone of every community. We believe it can be again.

The answer to a food system built on a few very large sites is one built on many small ones.

Closer

Meat processed where it is reared and sold close to where it is processed, within the farm's own community.

Richer

Farm income kept in the local economy, and a viable living for independent farmers on modest acreage.

Stronger

Many small, independent sites in place of a few large ones, so that the system bends rather than breaks.
