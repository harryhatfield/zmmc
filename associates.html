What It Is

A certified network for beef, lamb, pork and game.

Zero Miles Meat Company Associate is a certified network for beef, lamb and pork farms that meet our standard. It gives independent farms a badge worth having, a spot on our farm locator, and the marketing reach of a national brand, while staying completely independent businesses.

This sits separately from our poultry franchise. Poultry stays with our franchise network, where UK law allows the animal to be processed without ever leaving the farm. That exemption is for poultry only — beef, lamb, pork and game are never processed on-farm. Associate farms always work with a licensed, off-farm abattoir.

The Standard

Well above the legal minimum.

We built the standard the same way we built our poultry model: well above the legal minimum, backed by real numbers, not just a nice phrase.

  • Minimum 75 percent of diet from grass and forage across the animal's life, well clear of the 51 percent that lets a producer call itself grass-fed today
  • Rotational grazing, with paddocks rested for a minimum of 30 days between grazing passes
  • Full-time outdoor access, no permanent indoor housing
  • Pigs stocked at under half the recommended maximum outdoor density
  • Processed through small, local abattoirs wherever possible, or the closest available abattoir
  • Sold direct to the final consumer, or to a local retailer or caterer, never into national wholesale
