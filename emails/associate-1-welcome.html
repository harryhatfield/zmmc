Thank you for registering your interest in the Zero Miles Meat Company Associate Program. It's a certified network for beef, lamb, pork, game and turkey farms that meet our standard, giving you a badge worth having while you stay a completely independent business.

Here's a quick overview of how it works.

The Associate standard

✓ At least 75% of the animal's diet from grass and forage across its life
✓ Rotational grazing, with paddocks rested for at least 30 days between passes
✓ Full-time outdoor access, with no permanent indoor housing
✓ Pigs stocked at under half the recommended maximum outdoor density
✓ Processed through small, local abattoirs wherever possible
✓ Sold direct to the final consumer, or to a local retailer or caterer, never into national wholesale

What membership gives you

✓ Certified badge, backed by a standard well above the legal minimum
✓ Find a Hub listing, a spot on our farm locator alongside the Zero Miles hub network
✓ Marketing support, the reach of a national brand while your business stays independent

No franchise fee, no ongoing percentage of turnover and no equity. Just a simple membership.
