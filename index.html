What We Do

Butchery, reimagined in a shipping container.

Every Zero Miles hub is sited on or beside a partner farm. Meat is reared, prepared and sold within metres of where it's grown — cutting out haulage, wholesalers and food miles, without cutting corners on quality.

Farm-Direct Sourcing

Every hub partners with a named local farm. You know exactly where your meat comes from, because you can see the fields from the counter.

Converted Container Hubs

Compact, low-impact, fully-equipped units bring a proper butchery counter to communities without the cost of a full retail unit.

The Zero Miles Promise

Strength, stability and quality grounded in nature — our meat moves from farm to hub with as close to zero miles as possible.
