What We Do

Local meat, from the farmers who raise it.

Zero Miles connects people to meat from small-scale farmers in their own community — not an anonymous supply chain. Every farm in our network is independent, working to a standard well above the legal minimum, whether that's chicken from one of our on-farm poultry hubs or beef, lamb, pork and game from an Associate farm.

Small-Scale Producers

Every farm in our network is independent and small-scale, raising the bar on food quality. You'll always know exactly which farm — and which farmer — your meat came from.

On-Farm Poultry Hubs

For chicken, compact container hubs handle rearing and processing on-site — the on-farm exemption UK law allows for poultry only, so the animal never leaves the farm.

The Associate Standard

For beef, lamb, pork and game, our Associate Program certifies small-scale farms against real grazing and welfare standards, working with a licensed local abattoir.
