On-farm poultry for regenerative farms

Pasture-raised poultry that fits a regenerative system.

Pasture-raised chicken is a natural fit for regenerative and holistic farm management. The hard part has always been processing and selling it without the birds leaving the farm. Our on-farm poultry hubs are built to do exactly that.

On the pasture

Our poultry hubs keep birds out on pasture, every day, alongside what you already do on your land.

Processed on the farm

Compact container hubs handle rearing and processing on-site. It's the on-farm exemption UK law allows for poultry only, so the animal never leaves the farm.

Sold close to home

Whole pasture-raised chickens are sold direct from the farm gate, or through trusted local butchers and farm shops, instead of into an anonymous supply chain.

Farm diversification

A small footprint. Real diversification.

You're already farming (arable, livestock or mixed) and want to add a direct-to-consumer income stream without leaving the land or your day job behind. The Zero Miles hub turns a small footprint on your own farm into real diversification.

Our converted container hubs cost a fraction of a traditional farm building or retail fit-out, so you can launch faster with lower overheads, without retooling your whole operation.

FAQs

Common questions.

What's the investment required?

Full investment figures, and everything the investment includes, are in our Franchise Prospectus, which we email to you as soon as you request it. We'll also talk through ongoing operating costs openly during the application process.

Do I need my own farm to apply?

Yes. Our franchise model is built for existing working farms looking to diversify into direct-to-consumer meat sales. You don't need butchery experience, and full training is provided, but you do need land suited to a container hub.

Where does the hub go on my farm?

We work with you to choose the best spot on your own land, factoring in access, utilities and your existing operation, as part of onboarding.

How long does it take to open?

Timelines depend on site readiness and container build-out, and we'll confirm an estimate for your specific territory during the application stage.

Is my territory exclusive?

We plan hub locations carefully to give each franchise partner room to serve their community. We'll confirm territory terms as part of your franchise prospectus.
