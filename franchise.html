The Hub Model

One container. One farm. Processed on-site.

Site & farm partner

We match your territory to a local farm, and identify a site for the hub — on or near the farm, or in a well-placed community spot.

Container build-out

Your hub is fitted with rearing, packing and refrigeration equipment to our brand specification, ready to trade.

Trade as a Zero Miles hub

You run the hub day-to-day under the Zero Miles brand, selling whole pasture-raised chickens direct from the farm gate and to local butchers and farm shops.

Is This You?

Our ideal franchise partner.

You don't need a farming or butchery background — you need the drive to build something real in your community, and a genuine belief in what the Zero Miles way should mean: a chicken that never leaves the farm to be processed — the on-farm exemption UK law allows for poultry only.

  • Hands-on, community-minded, and ready to run a business day-to-day
  • Genuinely interested in sustainable, local food sourcing
  • Able to identify or already connected to a suitable site and/or farm
  • Comfortable following brand standards while building local relationships

FAQs

Common questions.

What's the investment required?

Costs vary depending on container specification, site and location. We share full, itemised investment figures in our Franchise Prospectus once we've had an initial discovery call.

Do I need farming or butchery experience?

No — full training is provided. We're looking for the right person to run the business and build local relationships more than prior farming experience.

Do you help find a site and farm partner?

Yes. If you already have a site or farm relationship in mind, we'll work with it — otherwise our team will help identify options in your target area.

How long does it take to open?

Timelines depend on site readiness and container build-out, and we'll confirm an estimate for your specific territory during the application stage.

Is my territory exclusive?

We plan hub locations carefully to give each franchise partner room to serve their community — we'll confirm territory terms as part of your franchise prospectus.
