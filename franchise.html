The Hub Model

One container. One farm partner. Zero miles.

Site & farm partner

We match your territory to a local farm, and identify a site for the hub — on or near the farm, or in a well-placed community spot.

Container build-out

Your hub is fitted with refrigeration, prep and counter equipment to our brand specification, ready to trade.

Trade as a Zero Miles hub

You run the hub day-to-day under the Zero Miles brand, serving farm-fresh cuts direct to your local community.

FAQs

Common questions.

What's the investment required?

Costs vary depending on container specification, site and location. We share full, itemised investment figures in our Franchise Information Pack once we've had an initial discovery call.

Do I need butchery experience?

No — full training is provided. We're looking for the right person to run the business and build local relationships more than prior butchery experience.

Do you help find a site and farm partner?

Yes. If you already have a site or farm relationship in mind, we'll work with it — otherwise our team will help identify options in your target area.

How long does it take to open?

Timelines depend on site readiness and container build-out, and we'll confirm an estimate for your specific territory during the application stage.

Is my territory exclusive?

We plan hub locations carefully to give each franchise partner room to serve their community — we'll confirm territory terms as part of your information pack.
