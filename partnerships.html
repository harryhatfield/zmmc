Unreasonable Procurement

Pasture-raised chicken from named British farms, into hospitality kitchens through Unreasonable Procurement.

Unreasonable Procurement (UP) is an outsourced procurement partner for hospitality businesses — pubs, restaurants and cafés, hotels, leisure and attractions, stadiums and events, contract catering, education and care — managing food, drink and indirect spend under one local, British supply-chain partner, from single sites to groups of 21+.

Through this partnership, our pasture-raised chicken — reared and processed on named British farms without ever leaving the farm — reaches hospitality kitchens through UP's supplier network, backing British farmers all the way from pasture to plate.
